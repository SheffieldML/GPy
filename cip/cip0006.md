## Summary

Restore correct behaviour in serialization, non-Gaussian likelihoods, predictive
quadrature, and `Y`-normalization, and leave runnable demos that show the
improvements. Contributor credit for the fix PRs belongs to **Raashish Aggarwal
(@raashish1601)**. Maintainer review, mathematical checks, verification figures,
CI deploy gating (#1134), and this CIP/demos packaging are by Neil Lawrence
(@lawrennd) with assistant support.

## Motivation

Several user-facing paths were silently wrong or unusable (wrong pickle types,
Gamma Laplace broken, Weibull samples mismatched to `logpdf`, NumPy-2 quadrature
failures, Bernoulli variational tails, normalizer scale mix-ups). The October
batch closed those gaps quickly. Without a CIP, that effort risks looking like
unrelated one-off PRs and under-credits the contributor who drove the fixes.
