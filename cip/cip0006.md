## Summary

Restore correct behaviour in serialization, non-Gaussian likelihoods, predictive
quadrature, priors, mean functions, grid inference, and `Y`-normalization.
Contributor credit for most fix PRs belongs to **Raashish Aggarwal
(@raashish1601)**. Maintainer review, verification figures, deploy gating
(#1134), Gamma observation-space moments (#1147), and this CIP/backlog packaging
are by Neil Lawrence (@lawrennd) with assistant support.

## Motivation

Several user-facing paths were silently wrong or unusable (wrong pickle types,
Gamma Laplace broken, Weibull samples mismatched to `logpdf`, NumPy-2 quadrature
failures, Bernoulli variational tails, normalizer scale mix-ups, prior
`rvs`/`lnpdf` bugs, missing mean-function gradients, broken grid Kronecker
order, and `predict` TypeErrors for several likelihoods). The October batch
closed those gaps quickly. Without a CIP, that effort risks looking like
unrelated one-off PRs and under-credits the contributor who drove most of the
fixes.

### Chain of related fixes (for readers)

1. **#1125** made Gamma + Laplace trainable (mean-rate `beta` gradients) but left observation-space `predict` unimplemented.
2. **#1146** fixed the generic sampling fallback signature so Exponential / Weibull / LogLogistic can `predict`; Gamma still failed (no `samples` / moments).
3. **#1147** adds Gamma moments and `samples` so `predict` works end-to-end.
4. **#1138** fixed single-output normalizer samples/LPD and noted multi-output `posterior_samples_f` as out of scope; **#1143** closed that gap.

Verification figures from review live under `docs/pr1135-*` and `docs/pr1136-*` on `devel`.
